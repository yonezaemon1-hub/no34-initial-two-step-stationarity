**Description (ready to paste):**

Unrefereed preprint. We exhibit an explicit connected eight-vertex, thirteen-edge all-odd-degree graph and an initial condition for synchronous neighbour-majority dynamics such that no vertex satisfies the two-step relation at every nonnegative time. This is the negation of the all-time initial-stationarity question in Section 6 of Benjamini, Zakharov and Zhukovskii (2026). Distinct even-time 0-stability is not refuted. Reproducible exhaustive calculations show vertex-minimality within the all-odd-degree model, edge-minimality at eight vertices, and a single isomorphism/sign-reversal class at eight vertices and thirteen edges. Mathematical witness and source code are included. Novelty and priority remain unestablished; the work has not been peer reviewed. All rights reserved.
